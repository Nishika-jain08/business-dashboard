About AlmaBridge

A career-readiness platform designed to connect learning, people and opportunities.

WHY ALMABRIDGE EXISTS

Career support should feel like a journey, not a collection of tabs.

AlmaBridge brings career direction, skill-gap recommendations, alumni mentorship, practical workshops and student-friendly opportunities into one profile-led experience. A student creates one profile, receives relevant next steps, connects with the right person, takes action and tracks progress — instead of repeatedly starting from scratch across different platforms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions students, mentors and businesses may have about AlmaBridge.

What is AlmaBridge?

AlmaBridge is an AI-enabled career and opportunity platform that connects students with personalised career guidance, skill-gap recommendations, alumni and expert mentorship, workshops and practical student-friendly opportunities.

Who can use AlmaBridge?

The core ecosystem has three active participant groups: students, alumni or industry experts, and businesses. Colleges and institutions can act as ecosystem and distribution partners.

How does the recommendation system work?

AlmaBridge currently uses transparent rule-based personalisation based on profile inputs such as target role, skills, interests, experience and location. The future production layer can move toward data-driven ranking after sufficient behavioural and feedback data is collected.

Can I book a 1-to-1 session with an alumnus or expert?

Yes. Select a mentor, choose an available time slot, select a payment method and complete the booking to receive a confirmation.

How does payment work?

AlmaBridge is designed for secure online payments. Payments are handled through the platform checkout flow, with booking confirmation generated after payment.

How does AlmaBridge make money?

The proposed model is hybrid and freemium: free core student access for acquisition, paid 1-to-1 mentorship and workshops through transaction or commission revenue, and business-side fees or subscriptions for project and talent access. These revenue assumptions still require validation.

What is the network effect?

More students can create demand for credible experts; more experts can increase student value; a stronger student talent pool can attract businesses; and more business opportunities can create additional value for students. These are platform hypotheses to validate, not outcomes already proven.

How will AlmaBridge get its first 1,000 users?

The proposed first-1,000 target is 800 students, 150 alumni or experts and 50 businesses, beginning with a bounded IBS Hyderabad pilot, student ambassadors, clubs and workshops, followed by curated alumni recruitment and a small business pilot.

Why would students use AlmaBridge instead of LinkedIn?

LinkedIn is a broad professional network. AlmaBridge is designed around a student-specific career journey: one profile, personalised next steps, mentor discovery, skill-building and practical opportunities in one workflow.

What has AlmaBridge validated so far?

The platform connects the complete student journey, and our survey provides early evidence of student interest. It does not yet prove scalable business demand, long-term retention, willingness to pay or population-level demand.
